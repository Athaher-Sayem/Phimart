02 SYSTEM NOTES

One backend.
Several moving parts.

The source tree is organized around distinct Django apps and versioned routes. These are code-backed building blocks, not claims about deployment or traffic.

A / PRODUCT
▦

Catalog and categories

Product, category, nested review and image routes are registered under /api/v1/.

B / ORDER
↗

Carts into orders

Cart items are nested under carts; orders have create, cancel and status-update actions.

C / ACCESS
⌁

Identity and docs

Djoser auth routes sit alongside generated Swagger and ReDoc endpoints in the Django URL configuration.
